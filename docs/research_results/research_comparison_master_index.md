SECTION 4. HOW TO USE THE COMPARISON SYSTEM CORRECTLY

Use it to:
- compare families without choosing a winner
- understand where two methods are truly similar
- see where one family is theoretically strong but operationally weak
- separate repo-fit from scientific attractiveness
- identify benchmark-dangerous families without dismissing them completely

Do not use it to:
- create a hidden final ranking
- turn grouped scores into a winner score
- ignore confidence levels or uncertainty comments
- bypass the benchmark-family constraints from governance and earlier research
