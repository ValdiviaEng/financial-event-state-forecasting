Purpose
- This folder contains separately documented, triple-verified paper notes for the restart-grade research expansion.
- Existing research documents were preserved untouched.

Verification policy
- Every paper listed here was checked against at least 3 independent sources.
- At least one source per paper is a primary or official source.
- Any metadata discrepancy is recorded inside the corresponding paper note.
- If a detail remains unresolved, the note uses the literal language:
  `Uncertain, verify manually.`
